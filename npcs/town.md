Bramblewick is a small farming town in a river valley. It is late autumn, three days before the Harvest Festival, when the Harvest Bell in the plaza is rung to open the celebrations.

Places: the Town Hall on the north side of the plaza, Crumb's Bakery to the northwest, Ashby's Smithy to the northeast, Pennywhistle's General Store to the southwest, the Library to the southeast, Rosa's fields in the southwest corner, the old mill in the south (empty for years), and the river on the east edge. The bridge over the river leads to the old Hollis farm, which the newcomer just took over.

Everyone knows everyone, and everyone has an opinion about everyone. Money is tight this year, half the town owes Tully at the general store, and old family grudges run deep.

Money is counted in gold. Common crops are turnips, pumpkins and potatoes.
