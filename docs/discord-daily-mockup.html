A day in Bramblewick

12:00 AM CentralThe director writes a new case. The bot posts it in #bramblewick-daily and opens a thread on it.
All dayPlayers question villagers in the thread: /talk, then just type. One villager thinks at a time; the thread locks while they do.
When you're readyPick the killer from the menu on the case post. Votes stay secret; you can change yours until midnight.
12:00 AM CentralMost votes is accused. The bot reveals the tally and the solution, locks the thread, and posts the next day's case.

How it works in Discord

  • One designated channel. The host runs /setup daily there. Players can't post in the channel itself, only in threads (Send Messages off, Send Messages in Threads on).
  • The case post is the ballot. It carries the briefing, a "Pick the killer" menu, and a vote card the bot edits as people vote: who has voted, never for whom.
  • The thread is the investigation. Everything from the current bot works there: /talk, typing, suggested-reply buttons, // table talk, and private /case, /people, /log.
  • Detectives are everyone who has spoken in today's thread. "4 of 6 have voted" counts them, and the bot notes in the thread when someone is ready.
  • At midnight the case post turns into the results, the thread gets the reveal and is locked and archived (still readable), and Day 13 starts.
  • On-demand mode (the current always-open channel) stays in the code for later; the bot runs daily mode for now.

Decisions to confirm

  1. Midnight "CST": midnight Central local time (CDT in summer, so 05:00 UTC then) or fixed UTC−6 all year? The mockup assumes Central local time.
  2. Ties: the mockup accuses whoever reached the top count first. Alternatives: nobody is accused (the killer walks), or a random pick among the tied.
  3. Secret ballot: hidden until midnight so people don't pile onto the leader. Or show live counts?
  4. Everyone ready early: reveal as soon as every detective has voted, or always wait for midnight?
  5. Laptop off at midnight: the reveal happens when the bot is next online, marked as late. The next case is written then too.
  6. No votes at all: reveal anyway ("the killer got away") and move on?
  7. Who may vote: anyone in the server, or only people who questioned someone that day?
